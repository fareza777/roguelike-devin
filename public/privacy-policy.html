Dreadmarch RPG

Privacy Policy · Last updated October 4, 2026

Dreadmarch RPG is a single-player dark-fantasy role-playing game published by the Dreadmarch team. This policy explains what information the Android app handles and why.

Information stored on your device

Your chronicle, settings, and local progress are stored on the device so the game can resume. We do not operate user accounts, a game server, or an in-app analytics system for this app.

Advertising

The app uses Google AdMob to show a banner, occasional interstitial ads, and optional rewarded ads. Depending on your region and consent choices, Google may process an advertising ID, approximate location, device information, and ad interaction information to provide, limit, measure, and prevent fraud in advertising. Consent and privacy choices are presented through Google’s consent tools when required.

Read Google’s Advertising Policies and Privacy information for details about Google’s processing. Rewarded ads are optional. A one-time purchase through Google Play is available to remove the app’s ads.

Purchases

Google Play processes the Remove Ads purchase, payment, and transaction records. Dreadmarch RPG does not receive or store your payment card details. The app uses the Play purchase result to unlock the entitlement on your device and to restore it when you reinstall or change devices through Google Play.

Children

Dreadmarch RPG is a dark-fantasy game intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data retention and deletion

To delete local game progress, use the “Erase save” control in Settings or remove the app from your device. Advertising and purchase records handled by Google are subject to Google’s retention and account controls.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy when the app’s data practices change. The effective date above will be updated with any revision. For questions, contact the project maintainer through the Dreadmarch RPG repository.
